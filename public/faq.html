常見問題 (FAQ)

Frequently Asked Questions

整理客戶最常詢問的開發相關問題，如果這裡找不到答案，歡迎聯繫我們諮詢。

Here are common questions about development. If you cannot find your answer, feel free to contact us.

開發一個專案通常需要多少時間？ How long does a project take?

項目週期取決於複雜度。一般中小型企業應用約需 4 至 8 週，而大型 AI 系統整合則需 3 個月以上。我們會在需求評估階段提供精準的時間表。

Project duration depends on complexity. Small‑to‑medium apps take 4‑8 weeks, large AI systems may take over 3 months. We provide an accurate timeline during evaluation.

你們提供售後維護與技術支援嗎？ Do you provide after‑sales support?

是的，所有交付的軟件均享有完善的售後保固期，並提供 7x24 的雲端監控與長期的技術升級維護方案。

Yes, all delivered software comes with warranty. We also provide 7×24 cloud monitoring and long‑term maintenance plans.

如何確保項目的程式碼安全與商業機密？ How do you protect code and business confidentiality?

我們在合作前會簽署嚴格的保密協議 (NDA)，且所有代碼資產在交付後全權歸客戶所有，確保最高的安全性。

We sign NDA before cooperation. All source code belongs fully to the client upon delivery.

App開發大約預算範圍是多少？ What is the budget range for App development?

簡單工具類App由數萬起，帶會員、支付、後台管理的商業App會依功能規模向上浮動。需要您提供功能清單，我們才可以給出精確報價。

Simple tool‑style apps start from tens of thousands. Commercial apps with membership, payment and backend vary by scope. Provide your feature list for an accurate quote.

做完之後原始程式碼會交給我嗎？ Will I get the source code after completion?

合約約定交付完成後，全部原始程式碼、設計源檔、數據庫腳本都會完整移交客戶，後續您可以找其他團隊繼續二次開發。

Upon delivery, full source code, design assets and database scripts are handed over. You may hire other teams for further development.

可以只做Android或者只做iOS單一平台嗎？ Can you build only Android or only iOS?

可以，支持只開發單一平台。多數商業項目建議雙平台同時開發，覆蓋更多用戶。

Yes, single‑platform development is supported. Most commercial projects recommend building both platforms for wider coverage.

App如何上架到Apple App Store以及Google Play？ How to publish App to App Store and Google Play?

需要客戶擁有對應開發者帳號，我們負責打包、上架素材準備、提交審核；審核由平台官方負責，我們協助處理審核被駁回的調整。

Client needs developer accounts. We handle build, assets and submission. Platform performs review, we assist on rejection fixes.

項目中途想要新增功能要如何處理？ What if I want new features mid‑project?

需求變更會另外評估工時與報價，簽署變更單後再進行開發；合約內原始範圍的功能免費調整。

Change requests are quoted separately after scope review. Original contract scope adjustments are free of charge.

還沒有完整產品構想，可以先諮詢嗎？ Can I consult without a complete product idea?

可以，歡迎免費前期溝通，我們協助梳理業務邏輯、建議可行技術方案，不用先付費。

Free pre‑project consultation available. We help sort business logic and propose technical solutions with no upfront payment.

是否可以先出原型，確認之後再寫程式？ Can you make prototype before actual coding?

可以，流程包含UI原型階段，畫面流程確認完畢，才進入實際編碼，避免後期大規模改動。

UI prototype phase is included. Coding starts only after prototype approval to avoid heavy rework later.

伺服器是你們提供，還是客戶自己購買？ Do you provide server or does client purchase it?

兩種模式都支持：可協助代租雲伺服器，也可以使用客戶本身已有的雲端帳號。

Both options available: we can help rent cloud servers or use client’s existing cloud accounts.

香港本地伺服器與海外伺服器如何選擇？ How to choose between Hong Kong and overseas servers?

主要用戶在香港就建議香港節點；面向全球用戶則選多節點CDN加速，我們會給出合適建議。

Choose HK node for local users; multi‑node CDN for global audience. We give proper recommendations.

你們可以做AI相關功能嗎？ Can you implement AI‑powered features?

可以，支援接入大語言模型、圖像識別、自動化流程、AI問答模塊，可整合進網站、App或是後台系統。

Yes. We integrate LLM, image recognition, automation and AI chat modules into websites, Apps and backend systems.

專案付款方式是怎樣？ What is your payment term?

一般採階段付款，簽約支付首期，階段交付對應階段款，全部驗收完畢支付尾數，具體會寫入合約。

Phased payment: initial payment on signing, milestone payments upon delivery, final payment after full acceptance. Terms stated in contract.

如果項目驗收發現bug如何處理？ How do you handle bugs after acceptance?

保固期內屬於合約範圍內功能缺陷，我們免費修復；新增功能、使用環境人為造成問題不在保固範圍。

Contract‑scope defects get free fixes within warranty. New features and man‑made environment issues are excluded.

可以接手別間公司做一半的爛尾項目嗎？ Can you take over unfinished projects from other vendors?

可以，需要先拿到源碼做代碼審查，評估架構品質，再告知是否可以接手以及對應成本。

Yes, source‑code review required first. We assess code quality then quote accordingly.

軟件完成後，會教我們員工如何操作後台嗎？ Will you train our staff for backend operation?

交付時提供操作說明文件，也可以提供線上講解教學，協助內部人員快速上手管理後台。

Operation documents are delivered. Online training sessions available for your team to get familiar with backend.

網站、App可以支援繁體中文、簡體多語言嗎？ Do websites and Apps support multi‑language?

原生支持多語言架構，繁體、簡體、英文等可以依需求配置。

Multi‑language architecture built‑in. Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese, English and others configurable on request.

開發完成之後後續版本更新如何處理？ How are future version updates handled?

可以簽長期維護合約，按月或按年，持續迭代新版本；也可以每次更新單獨報價。

Long‑term monthly/annual maintenance contracts for continuous iteration; one‑off quotation per update is also available.

你們的團隊技術棧是什麼？ What tech stack does your team use?

全棧團隊，覆蓋移動App、前端網頁、後端API、數據庫、雲端部署，一站式完成項目，不需要多方協調不同廠商。

Full‑stack team covering mobile App, frontend web, backend API, database and cloud deployment. One‑stop delivery without multiple‑vendor coordination.

什麼情況項目會延遲工期？ What causes project delays?

客戶反覆修改需求、確認回覆延遲、中途大量新增功能，都會影響工期，我們會及時同步風險。

Repeated requirement changes, slow feedback and mid‑scope feature additions cause delays. We keep you updated on risks.

可以先做Demo測試概念可行性嗎？ Can you build a Demo to verify concept feasibility?

可以製作最小可行性Demo，驗證商業概念，確認方向正確再投入完整開發。

Minimum viable Demo can be built to validate business idea before full‑scale development.
